## 1. Core Purpose (Invariant)

This project exists to make hiring a cleaner feel **personal, safe, and relieving**, not corporate or transactional.

The site should reduce decision anxiety by:
- showing a real local operator
- explaining the process plainly
- reframing cleaning as time and peace-of-mind regained

If a visitor feels “I could text this person and trust them,” the site has succeeded.

---

## 2. Unfair Advantage (Invariant for Public Projects)

Primary Unfair Advantage:
- **Owner-visible, hyper-local trust**
  The business is presented as a real person (Tanya), not a brand or franchise.

Secondary (optional):
- **Time-based framing instead of price-based framing**
  (e.g., “get your Saturday back” vs “$X per visit”).

Why this advantage matters to users:
- Cleaning requires access to private spaces.
- Trust and comfort outweigh small price differences.
- Users want reassurance more than feature lists.

Why it is difficult to replicate:
- Franchises rely on scale, scripts, and templates.
- Corporate brands cannot credibly present a single accountable human.
- Authentic local presence cannot be faked at scale.

---

## 3. Quality Bar (Invariant)

This project must meet or exceed top-tier expectations in:

- Clear, calm design (not flashy)
- Obvious next action (text/call)
- Fast load and simple navigation
- Mobile-first usability
- Local SEO and AI overview visibility
- Authentic copy (no corporate buzzwords)

Benchmarking competitors is allowed.
Imitating corporate tone or layouts is forbidden.
